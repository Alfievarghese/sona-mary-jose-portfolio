# Design System: Sona's Botanical Atelier

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
A prestigious synthesis of high-end editorial luxury, organic botanical serenity, and tactile hardware engineering. The visual language treats Sona Mary Jose's unique multidisciplinary mastery—bridging Embedded AI, IoT firmware, PCB circuitry, and refined UI/UX design—as an artisanal "digital conservatory." 

The atmosphere evokes a private glass conservatory at dawn: warm sunlit cream parchment, dewdrops resting on wild grasses, blooming blush peonies, and precision circuit traces etched like delicate botanical stems.
- **Density:** Level 4 (Breathes generously; spatial unhurriedness, minimum `py-24` to `py-36` section padding).
- **Variance:** Level 7 (Asymmetric editorial splits, double-bezel framed focal photography, bento masonry).
- **Motion:** Level 8 (Cinematic botanical curtain entrance using real transparent PNG illustrated meadow grass, wildflowers, and brush strokes parting sideways, accompanied by spring physics and floating petal physics).

## 2. Color Palette & Functional Roles
- **Petal Cream Canvas** (#FFF9F6) — Foundational background surface. Luminous warm paper tone with soft peach-blush undertone; completely eliminates sterile white glare.
- **Blossom White Core** (#FFFFFF) — Inner core container and card surface for maximum readability and contrast.
- **Rose Mist Tint** (#FDF3EE & #FAECE4) — Secondary alternating section background and nested card bezel shells.
- **Plum Charcoal Ink** (#241B21) — Primary editorial typography and heavy display headlines. Warm, ink-like depth that replaces cold `#000000`.
- **Dusty Sage** (#7D9585) — Secondary descriptions, captions, and biological balancing accents.
- **Blush Rose Accent** (#9E4A64 / #EAA2B7) — Single primary focal accent for interactive CTAs, active tags, and hover glows. Saturation strictly kept below 75%.
- **Meadow Green** (#4D6B57) — Hardware & IoT telemetry chips, firmware status dots, and organic indicators.
- **Warm Gold** (#C4943D) — Elite certifications (NPTEL Elite 69%), awards, and subtle highlight accents.
- **Whisper Shadow** (rgba(158,74,100,0.08)) — Ambient rose-tinted shadow; replaces cheap muddy drop shadows.

### Banned Colors
- Pure black (#000000) — strictly forbidden.
- AI Purple / Neon Cyan / Electric Violet — strictly banned.
- Hot pink or high-saturation magenta above 75% saturation.
- Cold corporate grays (#808080) — all neutrals must remain warm and biological.

## 3. Typographic Architecture
- **Display & Section Titles:** `Cormorant Garamond` (weight 600) — Scholarly, museum-curator grace with tight tracking (`-0.02em`).
- **Body & Technical Prose:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weight 300 & 400) — Open apertures, humanistic geometry, effortless scannability with relaxed leading (`1.75`). Max line width constrained to 65ch.
- **Botanical Script Accent:** `Caveat` (weight 500) — Handwritten organic annotations, artist notes, and section eyebrows.
- **Hardware & Telemetry:** `JetBrains Mono` (weight 500) — Microcontroller registers, pinouts, baud rates, and live IoT metrics.
- **Anti-Pattern Bans:** `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Arial`, `Times New Roman` are strictly banned.

## 4. The 2-Line Hero Iron Rule & Composition
- **H1 Line Restraint:** The primary headline uses a wide container (`max-w-5xl`) so it never wraps into an ugly 4–6 line block. It stays strictly at 2 concise, impactful lines.
- **Inline Botanical Micro-Pills:** Small, contextual illustrated botanical badges sit inline at type-height, acting as visual punctuation.
- **Asymmetric Split:** 55% editorial narrative on the left, 45% museum-grade portrait framing on the right.
- **Authentic Portrait:** Sona's real photo (`sona-photo.png`) is housed within a "Double-Bezel" (Doppelrand) architectural frame with concentric curves and delicate botanical corner accents.
- **CTA Architecture:** Dual-button action cluster:
  1. Primary Pill: Deep Plum Charcoal fill (`#241B21`), white text, nested circular trailing arrow badge that shifts diagonally on hover.
  2. Secondary Ghost Pill: Translucent blush glass fill with 1px rose hairline border.

## 5. The Signature Botanical Curtain Opening (Real PNG Artwork)
The opening animation is an organic botanical parting ceremony, NOT a solid plastic box:
- **Illustrated Art Assets:** High-resolution transparent PNG illustrations of lush wild meadow grass, blooming blush peonies, daisies, bellflowers, eucalyptus, and watercolor brush strokes.
- **Center-to-Sides Parting:**
  - Left botanical cluster (`curtain_left.png`) sweeps gracefully to the left (`transform: translateX(-110%)`).
  - Right botanical cluster (`curtain_right.png`) sweeps gracefully to the right (`transform: translateX(110%)`).
  - Bottom meadow grass & wildflowers (`meadow_bottom.png`) sinks gently downward (`transform: translateY(100%)`).
  - Delicate individual petals (`petals_cluster.png`) drift and float across the viewport with staggered spring physics (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` over 2.4s).
- **Completion State:** Once opened, the curtain overlay sets `pointer-events: none` and fades smoothly to `opacity: 0`, leaving the page completely interactive. A replay trigger allows visitors to re-experience the floral reveal anytime.

## 6. Component Mastery & Architectural Patterns

### Double-Bezel (Doppelrand) Architecture
Never place a card or image flatly on the background. All key elements use nested enclosures:
- **Outer Shell:** Soft rose-tinted container (`bg-[#FAECE4]/60`, `p-2`, `rounded-[2.5rem]`, `ring-1 ring-rose-200/50`).
- **Inner Core:** Clean Blossom White fill (`bg-white`, `p-7`, `rounded-[calc(2.5rem-0.5rem)]`, subtle inset highlight).

### Tactile "Button-in-Button" CTAs
- The trailing arrow (`↗`) is housed inside an independent circular chip flush with the button's right inner padding. On hover, the inner circle scales and shifts diagonally, providing haptic tactile feedback.

### Gapless Bento Grid
- Strict `grid-flow-dense` on the project showcase. 
- Real authentic projects from Sona's resume:
  1. **Smart Gas Stove (IoT & Safety)**: ESP32 WROOM, acoustic whistle-counting, automated gas valve shutoff, MQ-4 gas leak detection, local buzzer & mobile alerts.
  2. **JARVIS Offline AI Desktop Assistant**: TechAshy Hackathon 2026, Faster-Whisper offline voice model, LLaVA offline visual perception, glassmorphism Tkinter HUD.
  3. **Infotact Solutions UI/UX Case Study**: Remote design associate, Figma design system, wireframing, user flows, usability research.
  4. **IICT GmbH Germany & IISER Bhopal**: Embedded AIoT readiness, sensor physics, NPTEL Elite certified (69%).

### Interactive IoT & AI Telemetry Workbench
A live interactive widget on the portfolio demonstrating Sona's dual engineering prowess: visitors can toggle simulated stove burner telemetry, watch real-time acoustic whistle counter increments, and view offline AI speech waveforms.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
- **Cubic-Bezier Dynamics:** `transition: all 0.7s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` — organic spring mass physics.
- **Scroll Entry Reveals:** `IntersectionObserver` triggers smooth fade-up (`translate-y-8 blur-sm opacity-0` to `translate-y-0 blur-0 opacity-100`).
- **Floating Ambient Botanicals:** Micro-floating petal animations with parallax drift across background planes.
- **Hardware Acceleration:** All motion uses exclusively `transform` and `opacity`.

## 8. Anti-Patterns (Banned AI Clichés)
- No emojis anywhere in UI or code.
- No `Inter`, `Roboto`, or generic system fonts.
- No pure black (`#000000`).
- No neon / outer glow shadows.
- No generic 3-column equal card rows.
- No generic AI marketing buzzwords ("Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash", "Next-Gen").
- No fake round metrics (`99.9%`).
- No broken image links — all assets verified and stored locally.
- No filler prompts like "Scroll down to explore" with bouncing chevrons.
